HALO VR -- VEHICLE MESH REFERENCE

The names "hideMeshes" can use in halo_vr_vehcams.json. This file is documentation only: the mod never
reads it, and editing it changes nothing.

WHAT hideMeshes IS FOR
  Some vehicle cameras sit INSIDE the vehicle and see out through its hull. Anything the game draws in
  there shows up right in front of your eyes: the insides a damaged vehicle reveals, or a stray piece
  of geometry nobody could see in the flat game. hideMeshes hides parts of the vehicle while you sit in
  a seat, or only in one of its cameras.

HOW TO USE IT
  In halo_vr_vehcams.json, on a vehicle entry (every camera of that seat) or on one camera:

      "hideMeshes": ["canopy_m_core_damage", "canopy_m_panel"]

  - Each entry is TEXT FOUND IN A NAME below. Case does not matter. "canopy" hides every part with
    canopy in its name; "canopy_m_panela_damage" hides exactly one.
  - A camera's own list REPLACES its seat's list. "hideMeshes": [] on a camera hides nothing there.
  - Only parts of the vehicle itself are ever hidden, never your own body.
  - A part comes back the moment you change camera, get out, or press the kill switch.
  - Saved changes apply within a couple of seconds, so you can try names while sitting in the vehicle.

HOW THE GAME BUILDS A VEHICLE
  A vehicle is separate pieces hung on a skeleton. Each piece shows its STOCK mesh until that part of
  the vehicle is damaged, then the game swaps in a DAMAGED mesh. Some parts split into several pieces
  when damaged (the Banshee's canopy becomes a core and five panels). So:
  - To clear a view only when the vehicle is damaged, name the damaged meshes.
  - To remove a part outright, name both its stock and its damaged meshes.
  - From inside, a stock hull piece is usually see-through already, so hiding it changes nothing.

  The mesh marked "skeleton only" is the skeleton the pieces hang from. It draws almost nothing, but
  the Banshee's draws one stray triangle at the vehicle's centre, right in the eyes of a camera placed
  there: "sk_banshee" hides it. The other vehicles' skeletons have not been checked.

  A TURRET IS A VEHICLE OF ITS OWN in the game (the Scorpion's cannon, the Wraith's mortar, the gunner
  seats). A seat can hide parts of the vehicle it belongs to and of the hull its cameras follow.
  Hiding a turret's parts from a seat on the hull, or the hull's from some turret seats, is untested:
  if a name does nothing, that is the likely reason.

Names are the game's own, listed from the Steam release on 2026-10-04. A game update may
rename or add parts. Debris that flies off a vehicle is not listed: it is not part of the vehicle.
